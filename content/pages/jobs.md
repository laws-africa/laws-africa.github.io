- title: Administrative Coordinator
  description: 'Laws.Africa is hiring an Administrative Coordinator to run the day-to-day
    administration that keeps a small, remote team working, and to provide direct
    support to the Director of Partnerships. That means invoices and timesheets, quotes
    and supplier follow-up, calendars and meetings, contracts and onboarding, travel
    and events, and the shared drives and inboxes everyone depends on.


    Most of this work is currently done by managers and specialists in between their
    main jobs. We want one person to own it, run it the same way every month, and
    keep it visible so that nothing sits waiting in someone''s inbox.


    A lot of the job is closing loops. The invoice that hasn''t arrived, the quote
    nobody followed up, the meeting promised to a partner and never booked. If a tracker
    with nothing overdue gives you real satisfaction, we''d like to hear from you.


    You don''t need a legal background. You''ll learn how our editorial and digitisation
    teams work as you go.

    '
  responsibilities: '* Invoices and payments paperwork. Collecting and checking monthly
    invoices and timesheets from contributors, editors and data capturers against
    their contracts, rates and hours. Making sure each one carries the project, country
    and content type that Finance needs to charge the cost to the right funder, then
    passing them to Finance on a fixed monthly cycle. Processing expense claims against
    our policies.


    * Quotes, suppliers and subscriptions. Getting quotes, preparing side-by-side
    comparisons for the budget holder to decide on, and following up supplier invoices
    until they are paid. Keeping a register of the tools and subscriptions we pay
    for and when each one renews.


    * Calendars and meetings. Scheduling internal project meetings, sending invitations,
    adding new team members to recurring meetings, and taking notes and actions where
    needed.


    * Direct support to the Director of Partnerships.


    * Onboarding. Running onboarding and offboarding: email accounts, platform and
    drive access, time tracking, meeting invitations and the step-by-step guides new
    people need.


    * Travel and events. Booking flights, accommodation, venues and catering for team
    travel and Laws.Africa events, within budget and policy.


    * Shared drives, inboxes and access. Keeping shared drives organised and access
    correct, monitoring shared inboxes and routing what arrives, and handling access
    requests.


    * Trackers and processes. Keeping simple trackers for invoices, subscriptions
    and open requests so anyone can see what''s outstanding. Writing down how each
    process works, so that it doesn''t depend on one person''s memory, yours included.


    This role prepares, checks and follows up. It doesn''t approve spending, choose
    suppliers or keep the books. Budget holders approve and Finance pays, and your
    job is to make sure everything reaches them correct and on time.

    '
  way_of_working: '* We are a small team that values ownership and clear accountability.


    * We work remotely, across South Africa, Kenya and elsewhere.


    * We prioritise predictable delivery and follow-through over heroics.


    * We would rather hear about a problem early, with a proposed solution, than hear
    a reassurance that turns out to be wrong.

    '
  requirements: '* Two or more years in an administrative, office management, executive
    assistant or operations support role.


    * Experience processing invoices, purchase paperwork or expense claims, and the
    accuracy to catch a wrong rate, a missing timesheet or a duplicate invoice before
    Finance does.


    * Experience managing someone else''s calendar when it is busy and keeps changing.


    * Confidence with Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets) and
    the ability to pick up new tools quickly.


    * Clear written communication. Most of our coordination happens in writing, on
    Slack and by email.


    * The persistence to follow up without being asked twice, and the judgement to
    know when to escalate.


    * Discretion with contracts, rates and personal information.


    * The habit of working in an organised way: files where they belong, trackers
    kept current, nothing lost between tabs.


    * Based in South Africa or Kenya, with reliable internet and availability during
    South African working hours.

    '
  nice_to_have: '* Experience in a non-profit or on donor-funded projects where costs
    are tracked by funders.


    * Familiarity with Slack, Notion, DocuSign or time-tracking tools.


    * Experience arranging travel and events across African countries.


    * Basic design skills for simple social media graphics, using Canva or similar.
